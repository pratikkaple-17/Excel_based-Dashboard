# Adani Green Energy -- Renewable Energy Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an **interactive Excel dashboard for Adani Green
Energy Limited (AGEL)**, designed to analyze renewable-energy
generation, revenue, maintenance, plant performance, energy purchases,
waste generation, and state-wise performance.

The dashboard converts multiple raw-data tables into a business-focused
visual report that can help users understand operational performance and
identify important trends across **Solar, Wind, and Hybrid** power
plants.

> **Project Type:** Data Analytics / Excel Dashboard\
> **Tool:** Microsoft Excel\
> **Domain:** Renewable Energy / Energy Analytics\
> **Dashboard Period:** 2024--2026\
> **Plant Types:** Solar, Wind, Hybrid

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to build an interactive business
dashboard that provides a clear view of renewable-energy performance and
supports data-driven analysis.

The dashboard focuses on:

-   Energy generation performance
-   Revenue generated from energy operations
-   Maintenance cost and maintenance frequency
-   Plant-wise energy generation
-   Monthly energy-generation trends
-   Energy purchase by buyer
-   Equipment-wise maintenance cost
-   State-wise plant performance
-   Waste generation by plant type
-   Carbon-offset performance
-   Overall plant distribution and performance

## 📈 Important Visualizations

### 1. Energy Generation by Plant Type

The dashboard compares energy generation across:

-   Solar
-   Wind
-   Hybrid

In the displayed dashboard view, **Solar contributes the largest share
of energy generation**, followed by Wind and Hybrid.

### 2. Monthly Energy Generation

A monthly column chart is used to identify changes in energy generation
throughout the year.

This helps analyze:

-   High-generation months
-   Low-generation months
-   Seasonal patterns
-   Month-to-month variation

### 3. Waste Generation by Plant Type

A donut chart compares waste generation across Solar, Wind, and Hybrid
plants.

This provides a quick view of the relative contribution of each plant
category to waste generation.

### 4. Energy Purchase by Buyer

The dashboard compares energy purchased from different buyers,
including:

-   Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam
-   NHPC
-   NTPC
-   Rajasthan Rajya Vidyut
-   SEQA

### 5. Maintenance Cost by Equipment

Equipment-level maintenance analysis helps identify which equipment
categories contribute most to maintenance expenditure.

The dashboard includes:

-   Solar Panel
-   Transformer
-   Inverter
-   Wind Turbine
-   Battery

### 6. State-wise Performance

The state-wise summary combines operational and financial metrics such
as:

-   Total revenue
-   Number of plants
-   Maintenance cost
-   Carbon offset

This makes it easier to compare plant performance across different
states.
